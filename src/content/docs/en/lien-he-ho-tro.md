## A 2-minute self-check before you message

Many cases can be solved on the spot:

| What you are seeing                       | Quick check                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scan finds no phones                      | Did you add both address ranges and click **Active**? → [A4 · step 3](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a4-ngay-1-phan-cung)                                      |
| Phones show up but have no internet       | Is the proxy still alive? Is the time on the phone correct? → [A4 · step 3](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a4-ngay-1-phan-cung)                                |
| Many accounts get banned at the same time | Are any accounts sharing a proxy? → [A5](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a5-ngay-2-danh-tinh)                                                                   |
| A different app shows up after installing | Are you downloading from the links in the User Manual (HDSD)? Use the official APK folder in [A5 · step 3](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a5-ngay-2-danh-tinh) |
| The numbers do not move                   | Have you warmed up for the full 2–4 weeks? Did you pick the right niche? → [A7](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a7-ngay-4-5-nen-tang)                           |

Full list: [Troubleshoot by symptom](/en/tra-cuu-theo-trieu-chung).

## The 5 pieces of information to include

1. **Number of boxes** in use, and whether you use a **GenRouter H3000 or a mini PC**
2. Whether you are connected over **USB or OTG**
3. **Which day** of the roadmap you are on (Day 0–5) and **which step** you are doing
4. **How many phones Scan finds** / how many phones are running
5. A **screenshot** of the software at the moment of the error (and a screenshot of the phone if relevant)

## Message template — copy, fill in, send

```
[GenFarmer support]
- Number of boxes: ... (H3000 / mini PC)
- Connection: USB / OTG
- Currently on: Day ... – step ...
- Scan finds: ... / ... phones
- Problem: (what you were doing, what you see on screen)
- Already tried: ...
- Screenshot: (attached)
```

:::note
A message with all 5 items above is usually resolved in a single reply, instead of going back and forth several times.
:::
