> **Question of the day:** What can it produce for me?
>
> These two days are for becoming fluent, not for learning new concepts.

**Read your platform chapter → Configure for your content niche → Run a test script → Learn the signs that mean stop**

## Steps

### Read your platform chapter

Focus on the **configuration** section.

### Configure it for your actual content niche

Pick the wrong topic and your accounts will be warmed up as people interested in something else, and your posts will not reach the right audience.

### Run one complete script from start to finish

Read the results yourself.

### Learn to recognize the three signs that mean stop

1. Accounts are asked for verification more often than usual.
2. Posts suddenly lose all their views.
3. The number of running phones keeps dropping for no clear reason.

## Done when

:::tip
You can **configure a new script yourself** without opening the documentation again.
:::

:::note
From here on you are in the warming-up phase, which lasts **2–4 weeks**. During that time the numbers grow slowly, and that is normal. **Do not speed up to compensate.**
:::
