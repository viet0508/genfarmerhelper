Do this before the shipment arrives. The first three days are the most exciting; do not let them slip by while you wait to buy proxies.

> **Question of the day:** What do I need to have ready?
>
> No need to plug anything in yet. Just have every item below ready before you start Day 1.

**A place for the box + a dedicated power supply → Wired network + a control computer → Accounts and trial proxies bought in advance → An unboxing video**

## Preparation

* [ ] **A place for the box** — a well-ventilated or air-conditioned room, about 10 cm away from the wall so air can flow on all four sides. Do not put it on the floor, inside a closed cabinet, or next to a sunny window.
* [ ] **A dedicated power supply** — do not share a socket with an air conditioner, oven or washing machine. A UPS of 1,000 VA or more is recommended so the system does not shut down abruptly during a power cut. Have a **3-prong socket** ready if you do not have one.
* [ ] **A wired network connection** — one free port on your modem for the GenRouter.
* [ ] **LAN cable** (if you use a mini PC) — Cat5 or **Cat6 (preferred)**.
* [ ] **A control computer** — **Wi-Fi is not required**; a LAN connection works just as well. See the table below for how to connect it.
* [ ] **5 accounts and 5 proxies for a trial run** — buy them in advance, do not wait until you need them. You do not need 20 accounts yet.
* [ ] **vilao.ai API key** — used for automatic commenting. By default the script uses its built-in model; you can switch to another AI model available on vilao if you need to. The fee is about **2% ≈ 50,000 VND**, enough for generous use. Sign up and get your key at [vilao.ai/console](https://vilao.ai/console) — [step-by-step guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1f6s_gKAHptXNBoKldlCpfNDh0cWhd4l2/view?usp=drive_link).
* [ ] **Unboxing video** — film yourself opening the package, and keep the seals intact. This is your warranty evidence in case of a manufacturing defect.

### How to connect the control computer

| You use             | How to connect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **GenRouter H3000** | If your PC has Wi-Fi, connect over Wi-Fi. If not, use LAN: **H3000 LAN port → PC Ethernet**, or **Boxphone LAN → PC Ethernet** (in this case connect the H3000 LAN port to LAN1 or LAN2 of the Boxphone, then run the remaining LAN port to the PC — applies to the **P30** box). |
| **Mini PC**         | Connect over LAN. Wi-Fi **does not apply** to the mini PC. If your laptop has no Ethernet port, get a **USB-to-LAN** or **Type-C-to-LAN** adapter.                                                                                                                                |

## Three common mistakes

| ✕ | Mistake                                                                                      | Consequence                                                     |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | Putting the box in a closed cabinet or under a desk to keep things tidy                      | Heat cannot escape, the phones throttle themselves              |
| 2 | Sharing a power socket with high-power appliances                                            | The system crashes in the middle of a run                       |
| 3 | Buying a mini PC without a LAN cable, or using a laptop with no Ethernet port and no adapter | The shipment arrives and you still cannot connect your computer |
