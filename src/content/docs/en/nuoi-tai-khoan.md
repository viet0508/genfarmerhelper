After 5 days you have mastered the system. In this stage the system **runs itself**; your job is to **watch the right things at the right time — and leave alone what is working**. Results do not arrive in 5 days; they arrive here, after 2–4 weeks.

> **The question for this stage:** Are my accounts "alive", and what do I need to do each day?
>
> Ten minutes every morning. The rest is patience.

**10 minutes each morning → Read the signs → Do the week's job → Don't sabotage yourself → Message support at the right time**

## Every morning — 10 minutes

Same order, every day. Tick each item.

* [ ] **Open Control Center** — are **all 20 phones online**? For any that are off: re-plug power, wait 2 minutes, Scan again (see [Day 1](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a4-ngay-1-phan-cung)).
* [ ] **Open Schedules** — did last night's scenarios **run to completion**? Any schedule showing an error or stopping halfway (see [Day 3](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a6-ngay-3-tu-dong))?
* [ ] **Skim each phone** — is any phone **logged out**, **asking for verification**, or showing an unusual warning?
* [ ] **Write one line in your log** — date · phones online · accounts with odd signs · what you did. One line, but every single day.
* [ ] **Anything odd** → check the *Signs* table below. Not sure? [Message support](/en/lien-he-ho-tro) with the template and add "week … of warm-up".

:::note[What the log is for]
When something goes wrong, support's first question is **"since when?"**. With a log you answer in 5 seconds. Without one you guess — and fix the wrong thing.
:::

## Signs an account is alive or in trouble

| Alive — leave it alone | Watch out — act now |
| --- | --- |
| Stays logged in day after day | **Logged out unexpectedly** although nobody touched it |
| Scenarios run to completion every night | Scenarios **stop halfway** two nights in a row |
| No verification prompts | **Repeated verification prompts** within a few days |
| Engagement and views grow **slowly but steadily** | Numbers **completely flat** after 2 weeks, or a sharp drop |
| One proxy per phone, no duplicates | **Several accounts in trouble at the same time** |

:::danger[Several accounts dying at once]
Almost certainly **shared proxies** or a fault in the Router / Identity layer — not bad luck. Stop adding accounts, go back to [Day 2](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a5-ngay-2-danh-tinh), confirm one proxy per phone, then resume.
:::

## What to do each week

| Week | What you do | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Week 1** (right after Day 5) | Run only the **5 trial accounts**. Observe, log, **add nothing, change nothing**. | 5 accounts through **7 days** without incident |
| **Week 2** | 5 are stable → buy the rest and scale to **20 phones**, **one proxy per phone**. Repeat [Day 2](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a5-ngay-2-danh-tinh) for each new phone. | 20 phones online, scenarios run to completion |
| **Week 3** | **Keep the configuration unchanged.** If you have not picked a platform and niche yet, do it on [Day 4–5](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a7-ngay-4-5-nen-tang). | Numbers start to move **steadily** |
| **Week 4** | Review the log: which accounts are solid, which are weak. Decide what to keep, replace, or expand. | You can read your own log and know the next step |

:::tip[Why wait 7 days before scaling to 20]
Two calm days mean nothing — new accounts usually get scrutinised between day 3 and day 7. Scaling early multiplies the risk by four.
:::

## Don't do these

* **Keep changing settings** while numbers are flat — accounts need stability; change daily and you cannot tell what caused what.
* **Share proxies** to save money — the fastest way to lose a whole batch of accounts.
* **Tap around manually** on a phone that is running a scenario — mixed human/machine behaviour gets flagged.
* **Scale to 20 immediately** when the 5 trial accounts have not passed 7 days.
* **Install apps from links in the old manual** — use only the official APK folder from Day 2.

## When to message support

Message right away — do not "wait a few more days" — in any of these situations:

* **Two or more accounts** in trouble on the same day
* Scenarios **stopped two nights in a row** although you checked the schedules
* A phone **stays offline** after re-plugging power and scanning
* **Repeated verification prompts** with no clear cause

Use the [ready-made template](/en/lien-he-ho-tro) and add one line:

```
- Warm-up stage: week ... · trouble started on ... (from the log)
```

## Done when

:::tip[Done when]
20 accounts through **2–4 weeks** without a major incident, scenarios running every night, numbers growing slowly but steadily. Only now use the system for your goals on [Day 4–5](/en/a2-lo-trinh-mot-tuan/a7-ngay-4-5-nen-tang).
:::

## Three common mistakes

| ✕ | Mistake | Consequence |
| - | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tweaking scenarios every day out of impatience | Unstable accounts, and no way to tell which change caused the problem |
| 2 | Scaling to 20 after the 5 trial accounts were calm for only 2 days | Not enough data — risk of losing the whole batch on days 3–7 |
| 3 | Keeping no log | Cannot answer support's "since when?", so the wrong thing gets fixed |
